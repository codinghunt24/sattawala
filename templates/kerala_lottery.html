About Kerala State Lottery

The Kerala State Lottery Department was established on September 1, 1967, making it the first government lottery in India. The lottery was introduced by the then Finance Minister of Kerala, PK Kunju Sahib, with the aim of providing employment and generating revenue for the state.

Types of Kerala Lottery

Kerala conducts 7 weekly lottery draws throughout the week. Each lottery has a different name, prize structure, and ticket price. The weekly lotteries include Win-Win, Sthree Sakthi, Fifty Fifty, Karunya Plus, Nirmal, Karunya, and Akshaya.

Frequently Asked Questions - Kerala Lottery

What time does the Kerala Lottery result come out? +
Kerala Lottery results are announced daily at 3:00 PM IST. The live draw takes place at Gorky Bhavan, Near Bakery Junction, Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala. Results are usually available on this page within minutes of the draw.
Which Kerala Lottery draws happen on which day? +
Monday: Win-Win (W), Tuesday: Sthree Sakthi (SS), Wednesday: Fifty Fifty (FF), Thursday: Karunya Plus (KN), Friday: Nirmal (NR), Saturday: Karunya (KR), Sunday: Akshaya (AK). Each lottery has a different prize structure and ticket price.
How to claim Kerala Lottery prize? +
Prizes up to ₹5,000 can be claimed from any authorized lottery agent. Prizes between ₹5,001 to ₹1,00,000 can be claimed from the District Lottery Office. Prizes above ₹1,00,000 must be claimed from the Directorate of State Lotteries in Thiruvananthapuram with original ticket, valid photo ID, and 2 passport-size photos within 30 days.
Is Kerala Lottery legal? +
Yes, Kerala Lottery is 100% legal and is operated by the Kerala State Government under the Directorate of Kerala State Lotteries. It was established in 1967 and is regulated under the Kerala Paper Lotteries (Regulation) Rules, 2005. It is one of the most transparent lottery systems in India.
What is the tax on Kerala Lottery winnings? +
Lottery winnings above ₹10,000 are subject to 30% TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) plus 4% Health & Education Cess, making the effective tax rate approximately 31.2%. The tax is deducted at source before the prize amount is paid to the winner.
Can I buy Kerala Lottery tickets online? +
Currently, Kerala Government does not sell lottery tickets online officially. Tickets can only be purchased from authorized lottery agents and outlets across Kerala and some other states where lottery is legal. Be cautious of fraudulent online lottery sellers.

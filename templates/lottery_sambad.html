About Lottery Sambad

Lottery Sambad is a collective name for the state lottery draws conducted by Nagaland, West Bengal, and Sikkim. The 1:00 PM draw is conducted by Nagaland State Lottery, the 6:00 PM draw by West Bengal State Lottery, and the 8:00 PM draw by Sikkim State Lottery. Each draw operates independently under the provisions of the Lotteries (Regulation) Act, 1998.

Tickets are priced at ₹6 each and are available at authorized lottery agents across these states. The lottery program generates significant revenue for state welfare programs and provides employment to thousands of lottery agents and distributors.
